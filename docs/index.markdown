# About Me

Hello! I'm currently an undergraduate senior majoring in Neuroscience at Princeton. I am working in the Niv Lab under graduate student Branson Byers, studying how planning contributes to human goal persistance (i.e. whether having to plan increases the likelihood for us to stick with our goals, even when abandoning them may be more advantageous).

My research interests include computational cognitive science, reinforcement learning, and goal-directed planning.
